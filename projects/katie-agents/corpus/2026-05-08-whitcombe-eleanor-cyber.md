# 8 May — Whitcombe LLP

**Who:** Eleanor Strand
**Where:** their offices, sandwiches

She had more to say this time, within limits.

- Running a coverage dispute that turns entirely on whether an incident was state-backed. Wouldn't name it.
- "The policy says excluded if attributable to a state actor. Nobody has defined attributable. The insurer has a government advisory saying probably. The insured has an expert saying possibly not. And the policy gives you no mechanism to decide between them — no arbitration, no deeming provision, nothing. It is a hole in the middle of the wording."
- Says she has two more of these behind it.
- Her view: the market will keep writing the ambiguity until a judgment forces a change, and that's years away.
- She thinks buyers have no idea.

This is the second time in six weeks. Fern said almost exactly the same thing from the underwriting side.
