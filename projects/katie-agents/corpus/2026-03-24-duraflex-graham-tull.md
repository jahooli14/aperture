# 24 Mar — Duraflex Industries

**Who:** Graham Tull (Group Risk Manager)
**Where:** Coventry, their site

Worth the train. Graham is exactly the kind of buyer who tells you what's actually broken.

- Programme renews 1 October. Property, casualty, marine cargo, some product recall.
- The problem is battery storage. They've put lithium-ion storage into four sites as part of the energy strategy and the insurance market has, in his words, "gone into a bunker about it".
- "I have four sites with battery storage and I cannot get anyone to write it properly at a sane price. I'm buying a sublimit that doesn't cover the loss I'm actually worried about and paying through the nose for the privilege."
- Current cover is a £5m sublimit inside the property programme. He reckons the real exposure is north of £40m on one site alone.
- Said it plainly: "if anyone will write this properly I'll move the whole programme to them. The whole thing."
- Also grumbled about a marine cargo claim taking nine months. Separate issue.

Action: who writes BESS properly? Ask around. He is serious about moving.
